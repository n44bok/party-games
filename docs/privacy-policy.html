Privacy Policy

Party Games · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Party Games (“the app”) is a collection of party games for a group of friends who share one phone. This page explains what information the app uses and why. In short: we do not have accounts, we do not run a server for the app, and we do not collect or sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data is Google, to show ads (see “Ads” below).

Who is responsible

The developer of the app: Oleksandr Romanov. Contact: n44bok@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • Player and team names you type for a game are used only during that game and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Settings (language, vibration, the time warning choice, and whether ads were removed) are stored on your phone only.

Microphone (Song Backwards)

The game “Song Backwards” records your voice with the microphone, plays the recording backwards and plays it back to you. Recordings are processed on your phone only. They are never uploaded, shared or used for anything else, and they are deleted from the phone’s temporary storage right after they are read. The app asks for microphone permission the first time you record; you can turn it off at any time in your phone’s settings (the other games work without it).

Ads

The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information from them in a form that identifies you.

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app asks for your choice with Google’s consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy (shown where it applies).
  • iPhone and iPad. The app asks for your permission before tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are less personalised, and every game works as usual.
  • You can also reset or limit the advertising identifier in your phone’s settings.

More about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

If the app offers a one-time purchase to remove ads, the payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not see or store your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union), and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Some game cards contain adult humour.

Your rights

Because we do not keep personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data that Google collects for ads, you can use your consent choices described above and the controls in your Google account. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the rights set out in the GDPR, such as access, deletion and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes
